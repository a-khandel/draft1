Relay combines the best of async messaging, task management, and voice — in one beautifully simple product.

Async Voice

Send rich voice messages that teammates can listen to on their own schedule. No more forcing everyone into a meeting to share a quick update.

  • Auto-transcription included
  • Emoji reactions & replies

Smart Tasks
